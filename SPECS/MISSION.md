# Mission

## Purpose

A simple **cafe ordering system** for customers. A customer can browse the
menu, build a cart, go through checkout, and get confirmation that their
order has been sent to the kitchen.

## Who is it for

People ordering at the cafe.

## What success looks like

- **Easy to navigate** — the customer can find their way around without
  thinking about it.
- **Visually appealing** — the menu and cart look nice.
- **Item descriptions (and pictures)** — every menu item is shown with a
  description and a picture.
- **Easy to use** — adding to the cart, changing the cart, and checking out
  are all simple.
- **Functioning** — the whole flow actually works, from menu to confirmation.

## Non-negotiables

- It must be **simple** — easy for learners to read and build.
- It needs a **secure payment** step — but since we are not connecting to a
  real bank, we use a **simulated checkout** with fake card details.
- It must **actually work** end to end.

## Out of scope (for now)

- A separate **kitchen screen**. "Send to kitchen" is only a confirmation
  step for now.
